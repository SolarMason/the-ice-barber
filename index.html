Our Story

A NEPA family business — built on great events.

The Ice Barber is a proudly family-owned and operated mobile shaved-ice service rooted in Northeast Pennsylvania. We've spent countless weekends at local events, fairs, parties, and community gatherings, and we know what makes a treat lineup unforgettable: real flavor, real care, real smiles.

We started The Ice Barber to bring a touch of summer joy to every event we serve. Whether it's a child's birthday, a corporate cookout, a school festival, or a sun-soaked sports tournament, we show up prepared, professional, and ready to make a moment kids and grown-ups will remember.

And we don't cut corners on what goes in the cup. Our nectars are made from scratch in our own kitchen with fresh-squeezed real fruit — never artificial syrups, dyes, or high-fructose corn syrup. It's the kind of difference parents notice on the first taste.

Everything we do is local — we live here, we play here, and we love making our neighbors' events even better. From Lackawanna County to the Poconos and across the NEPA region, you'll spot our setup at the community events that matter.
