Print recommendations

These designs are optimized for recycled, uncoated, cream-toned paper stocks. Here are the specs and shops we recommend.

Recommended paper stocks

Cream uncoated stocks bring out the brand's warmth and let the eco design shine. Look for high post-consumer recycled content (PCW).

100% PCW

Mohawk Loop

Premium PCW recycled. Cream and ivory shades available. Great feel for cards and flyers.

Distribution tips

  • 1
    Business cards work everywhere — give a stack to every venue, vendor, and event partner you work with. Always carry some in your wallet.
  • 2
    Tear-off flyers live on community bulletin boards: laundromats, coffee shops, libraries, grocery stores, gym corkboards. Bring a thumbtack.
  • 3
    Door hangers are perfect for targeted neighborhoods near upcoming events — check zoning rules, never put in mailboxes (USPS violation).
  • 4
    Postcards can be hand-delivered or mailed. EDDM (Every Door Direct Mail) through USPS is cost-effective for blanket campaigns by ZIP route.
  • 5
    Rack cards live in tourist info displays, hotel lobbies, and visitor centers across NEPA. Hand-deliver a stack and refresh every few months.
  • 6
    Sales sheets are for B2B — leave with banquet managers, wedding planners, school activity coordinators, and event center directors as a take-away.
  • 7
    Posters get attention in high-foot-traffic windows: pizza shops, barber shops, ice cream stands, breweries. Trade with other small businesses.
